# Coachwhip 0.5.2

Mac mini M4, 16 GB, measured; your Mac and your prompt will move the numbers.

## Faster on every Qwen3-Next and Qwen3.5/3.6 MoE model

- **The attention block of a linear-attention layer is now one kernel per word.** Three layers in four of these models use gated delta-net attention, and it was built from about forty small GPU operations per layer: the convolution taps, the activation, two norms, the widening, the delta rule over the memory, the output norm and the gate. They are now one Metal kernel per word; the two projections stay as their own matmul kernels. The arithmetic is the same: on the exact path the answers are byte-identical to 0.5.0.
- **Measured, fast mode, raw 300 tokens:** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B squeezed (bank 68) 14.0 → 16.4 tok/s; Qwen3-Coder-Next squeezed (bank 109) 11.9 → 14.3 tok/s. In the crew demo (four agents, a cache with eight executed checks) every seat on the 35B wrote at 16.4 to 17.0 tok/s.
- `COACHWHIP_ATTN_KERNEL=0` restores the old operation-by-operation path.

## Where the time went, for the curious

On a 16 GB Mac the SSD stopped being the clock once experts were squeezed and stood in: a 35B word cost about 60 ms, only 10 of them in the expert layers. The rest was the attention side, and the delta step alone was 13 ms of it. The kernel takes most of that back.

## Not tested

- Macs other than an M4 with 16 GB.
- gpt-oss-120b is unaffected (a different attention); its numbers are 0.5.0's.
- Prompts past 16K tokens.
