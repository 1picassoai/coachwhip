# Coachwhip 0.5.1

Mac mini M4, 16 GB, measured; your Mac and your prompt will move the numbers. The engine binary is unchanged from 0.5.0; this release is a model file, a setting, and the words.

## The 80B coder, ready-squeezed

- **Qwen3-Coder-Next, 80 billion parameters, 3 billion active, on a 16 GB Mac at about 13 tok/s.** The machine cannot hold the 38 GB file; Coachwhip streams the experts it needs and keeps the rest asleep.
- **Download the squeezed file instead of squeezing it yourself.** `Qwen3-Coder-Next.Q2X.gguf`, 29,335,336,000 bytes, sha256 `cdc0584c1e4d30134cbb83f18e4c7607af90bd1145a64940c7a357b02a0216af`, made by the shipped `coachwhip-prepare` from MaziyarPanahi's Q3_K_M (gate and up experts to Q2_K, down to Q3_K; everything else untouched). Link: (Hugging Face, under the author's account; filled in at publish). The installer checks the hash before it runs.
- **One line to start it**, bank 109, fast mode on: `~/coachwhip/coachwhip.sh ~/Downloads/Qwen3-Coder-Next.Q2X.gguf ~/Downloads/tokenizer.json 109 8090 --parallel --flash-reads --stand-ins`

## Measured, same prompt, same Mac

| setup | writing | quality on the retry-helper prompt |
|---|---|---|
| plain Q3_K_M file, bank 70 | 5.4 tok/s | 5 of 5 |
| squeezed file, stand-ins on, bank 109 | 13.5 tok/s | 4.5 of 5 |

Raw 300-word runs: 6.2 plain, 10.4 with stand-ins on the plain file, 11.9 squeezed with stand-ins. Bytes read from the SSD per 300 words: 31 GB, 6 GB, 5 GB. Fast mode changes the answers: the half point was two faults in the usage example, one an undefined function; the loop and the error handling were right. ## Changed words

- `--stand-ins` was listed as "not tested" on the Qwen models in 0.5.0. It is now measured on Qwen3-Coder-Next, above. Still not measured: the 122B and the 35B with stand-ins.

## Known

- Streaming calls to `/v1/chat/completions` with gpt-oss return the model's thinking inline as a `<think>` block in `content`; plain calls return the final answer only. gpt-oss only; the Qwen models are unaffected. Fix in the next engine release.

## Not tested

- The squeezed coder on Macs other than an M4 with 16 GB.
- Chats past 16K tokens.
